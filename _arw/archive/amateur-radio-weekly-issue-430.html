Editor's Corner

Hi, my name is Cale (K4HCK) and I'm the editor of Amateur Radio Weekly.

The most-clicked link from last week's newsletter was the plea from MeshCore to provide financial support in their effort to avoid a potential trademark claim that could further split the project.

The top story in today's issue points readers to HAMgpt, an AI interface to all things Ham Radio. If you haven't jumped into any AI based tools, this is an easy one to get familiar with. The dashboard is exceptionally helpful. I've shared with Rodrigo (AK6FP / LU6ERV) a feed of nets from Net Finder, which allows HAMgpt users to ask questions like “Are there any radio nets on the air right now?” or “Tell me about the Maritime Net.” This was a fun collaboration and I encourage you to check it out. Rodrigo also maintains DXLook, an excellent live map of Amateur Radio activity and propagation.
