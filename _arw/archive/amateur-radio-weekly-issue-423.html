Editor's Corner

Hi, my name is Cale (K4HCK) and I'm the editor of Amateur Radio Weekly.

The most-clicked link from last week's newsletter was Off Grid Ham's comments about the plain old telephone service (POTS) coming to an end. POTS is dead at my house. We recently did a kitchen remodel and the drywall contractor walled over the old phone plug no questions asked. In my mind the ISDN line was the pinnacle of POTS. I pined over 128 kbit/s of fat bandwidth for many years.
