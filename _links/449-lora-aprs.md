title: LoRa APRS
excerpt: LoRa APRS uses long range, low power chirp spread spectrum modulation to encode Automatic Packet Reporting System data packets over radio. This technology uses much less power than traditional APRS, and generally travels longer distances. It also enables the use of much smaller, cheaper equipment, and allows for easier setup.
