[Churches and Chapels on the Air](https://wacral.org/chota/) (CHOTA) will be active on Saturday September 12th. CHOTA was first established in 2013 and encourages fellowship among both activators and chasers.

A [list of participating stations](https://wacral.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CHOTA-2026-at-11-July.xlsx) (XLSX) is available on the CHOTA 2026 website. CHOTA is managed by [The World Association of Christian Amateurs and Listeners](https://wacral.org/) (WACRAL).
