*The following is a message from the producers of Silent Key podcast:*

I'm Cameron, and my partners and I just launched our first audio drama. It's called [Silent Key](https://shows.acast.com/silent-key), and yes, it means exactly what you think it does.

The story follows Lena Allan, who heads back to the remote island where she grew up after her dad, a ham operator, becomes a silent key. Nobody's quite sure how he died. His station is still sitting in the shack, and it keeps picking up transmissions it shouldn't. His logbook and a box of old tapes become her only way to figure out what happened to him.

We had a lot of fun putting the hobby front and center: call signs, logbook entries, on-air etiquette, and that feeling of hearing a voice come through the static late at night. It's a horror show, so it gets creepy, but there's a lot of love for radio in it.

It's 12 episodes with a full cast, and a new one drops every Thursday through the new year. It's free on Apple Podcasts, Spotify and wherever you listen: [https://shows.acast.com/silent-key](https://shows.acast.com/silent-key)

If you give it a listen, I'd love to hear what you think. And if we got any radio details wrong, please tell us!

73,   
Cameron
