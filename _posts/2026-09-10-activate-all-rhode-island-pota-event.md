[Activate All RI](https://ripota.org/activate-ri-2026/) aims to get all 61 of Rhode Island [POTA](https://parksontheair.com/) parks on the air September 11–13. Think of it as a POTA take on a state QSO party: get a bunch of us out operating and give everyone else a weekend of Rhode Island parks to chase. Activating every park in a state over one weekend is one of those things we can actually tackle because we’re so tiny. Might as well put that to good use!

We start at 0600 EDT Friday September 11th and end 1800 EDT September 13th.

For hunters, it’s a chance to fill in missing parks toward Worked All RI, or just chase a few of us from home or park-to-park. The [website](https://ripota.org/activate-ri-2026/) has an [activation schedule](https://ripota.org/activate-ri-2026/schedule/), [live RI spots](https://ripota.org/on-air/), and a tool that lets hunters import their POTA hunted-parks history and get a schedule for just the parks they still need.
