[ARISS](https://www.ariss.org/) has [announced](https://mastodon.hams.social/@ARISS_Intl/117440976261016796) an upcoming SSTV event transmitted from the International Space Station (ISS) beginning on October 22nd and continuing through October 25th. This is SSTV Series 34 and will feature images of ARISS hardware.

**Dates**   
October 22 through October 25

**Mode**   
Robot 36

**Frequency**   
437.500 MHz +/- Doppler shift

Received images may be submitted to the [ARISS SSTV Gallery](https://ariss-usa.org/ARISS_SSTV/).
