[Amateur Radio Daily](https://daily.hamweekly.com/) (and [Amateur Radio Weekly](https://hamweekly.com/)) will attend [Zero Retries Digital Conference](https://www.zeroretries.org/p/conference) (ZRDC) and [Pacificon](https://www.pacificon.org/) October 16th through October 18th. My name is Cale (K4HCK) and I produce both publications. I'll be speaking at ZRDC about *New Tech Hams*, an emerging group of hams that are highly focused on digital communication and the software that powers it. Thanks to ZRDC for the invitation! I think this talk will be a lot of fun. 

[Tickets](https://lp.constantcontactpages.com/ev/reg/8zyqbad) are still available for ZRDC both for in-person and Zoom based attendees. ZRDC focuses on technical innovation in amateur radio. The speaker line-up includes representatives from [FreeDV](https://freedv.org/), [DigiPi](https://craiger.org/digipi/), [M17](https://m17project.org/), and more. [Learn more about ZRDC](https://www.zeroretries.org/p/conference).

I will also be attending Pacificon and will share a booth with [Zero Retries](https://www.zeroretries.org/). Stop by booth 33 and say hello! I look forward to meeting you and hearing your feedback. [Tickets for Pacificon](https://www.pacificon.org/registration/attendees) are also still available.

73 and thanks for reading!
