[Icom](https://www.icomamerica.com/lineup/amateur/) has received a [Good Design Award](https://www.g-mark.org/en/) for its latest [ID-5200](https://www.icomamerica.com/lineup/products/ID-5200A/) transceiver. 

The Good Design Award judges outlined three reasons for choosing the ID-5200:

1. Equipped with independent dials for left and right, enabling intuitive operation when receiving two waves simultaneously.
2. A modern design centered on a color touchscreen, blending seamlessly with car interiors.
3. A separated main unit/controller structure and a variety of mounting options make it adaptable to any installation environment.

The Good Design award describes itself as:

> [The] only comprehensive design evaluation and promotion mechanism in Japan. Together with G Mark, it has been recognized by a wide range of people for nearly 70 years, and has continued its activities to make people's lives and society better through design.

This is the second year in a row that Icom has won the award. Last year the Icom IC-7760 took home a Good Design Award.
