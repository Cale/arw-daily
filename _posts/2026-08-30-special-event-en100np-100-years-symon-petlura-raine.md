*The following is a message from UR5NBC:*

Special event callsign [EN100NP](https://www.qrz.com/db/EN100NP) for the 100 Years memory Symon Petlura will be active till 31 December 2026 from Vinnytsia, Ukraine.

> EN100NP is the special event callsign for the 100 Years memory Symon Petlura an outstanding Ukrainian statesman, politician and military figure, Chairman of the Directory of the Ukrainian People's Republic (UNR) and Chief Ataman of the Army and Navy of the UNR. Callsign will be operating from the 8 June till 31 December 2026.

In order to receive the associated diploma, hams will need to gather 100 points worth of QSOs from various Ukrainian stations. More information is available on the [UR4NWW website](https://ur4nww.vntu.edu.ua/en100np/).
