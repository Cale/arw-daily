*The following is a press release from the Dan Beard Council Radio Scouting Committee:*

CINCINNATI - Fifty-eight Scouts from across the Dan Beard Council got on the air October 17 and 18 as part of Jamboree on the Air, the largest Scouting event in the world.

Held every third full weekend of October since 1958, Jamboree on the Air connects more than a million Scouts in over 150 countries using amateur radio. Scouts swap greetings, compare notes on camping and school, and make friends on other continents without leaving home.

The committee ran two sites across the weekend. Forty Scouts worked the station at the National Voice of America Museum of Broadcasting in West Chester, co-hosted with the West Chester Amateur Radio Club as WC8VOA. Another eighteen operated from a campout at Camp Friedlander in Loveland under the council callsign KY4BSA.

For Scouts from Troop 674 in Montgomery, the weekend was the finish line of a program that started weeks earlier. Volunteer counselors taught three classroom sessions at troop meetings covering radio safety, how radio waves travel, regulation, and careers in the field. The campout supplied the part no classroom can: a Scout with a microphone in hand, calling out and waiting to see who answers.

The event also counts toward advancement. Participating in Jamboree on the Air now satisfies a requirement for the Citizenship in the World merit badge, and the weekend covered the hands-on portion of the Radio merit badge for Scouts in the Troop 674 program.

### More Chances to get on the Air This WInter

The Troop 674 program was a dry run. The committee will teach the Radio merit badge at the council's Merit Badge Challenge in 2027, with sessions at Fairfield High School on January 16 and 30, and in Northern Kentucky on March 6 and 20. Before that, the committee is working with clubs in our area to host Scouts for Winter Field Day, January 23 and 24, where it plans to offer Radio merit badge sign-offs for Scouts who need the on-air requirement.

### About the Radio Scouting Committee

A volunteer group of licensed amateur radio operators who bring equipment and instructors to Scout units across southwest Ohio and northern Kentucky at no cost to the unit. The committee has put 255 Scouts and 61 adults on the air since January across thirteen activations. More licensed operators are welcome; no teaching experience is required.
