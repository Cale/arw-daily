The [International Amateur Radio Association](https://www.aira.pt/) (AIRA) has announced [its second technical event](https://www.aira.pt/en/events/segundo-evento-tecnico-aira), focusing on the intersection of amateur radio and emergency response. The special event, titled *"Emergency Communications: The Role of Amateur Radio in Civil Protection,"* is scheduled to take place virtually on Friday, November 20, 2026, from 9:00 PM to 10:30 PM.

The session will feature guest speaker João Paulo Saraiva (CT1EBZ), representing APROSOC (Civil Protection Association). The presentation aims to highlight how radio operators and emergency response teams can collaborate effectively, underscoring the event's theme: *"Amateur Radio and Civil Protection: A partnership at the service of society!"*

Organizers emphasize that the gathering is designed to promote knowledge sharing, discussion, and cooperation among participants.
