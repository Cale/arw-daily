[ARISS](https://www.ariss.org/) has [announced](https://mastodon.hams.social/@ARISS_Intl/117315559694748769) an upcoming SSTV event transmitted from the International Space Station (ISS) beginning this coming weekend on October 2nd and continuing through October 6th. This is SSTV Series 33 and will be the first SSTV event from ISS [Expedition 75](https://www.nasa.gov/mission/expedition-75/). The image content is expected to highlight student education.

**Dates**   
October 2 through October 6

**Mode**   
Robot 36

**Frequency**   
437.500 MHz +/- Doppler shift

Received images may be submitted to the [ARISS SSTV Gallery](https://ariss-usa.org/ARISS_SSTV/).
