*By Robert B. Davidheiser, Sr., Ph.D. (W5RBD)*

A radio station is more than a collection of equipment. Its operator knows which cable goes where, how the accessories work together, and what has changed over the years. Much of that knowledge may never have been written down.

[Signal & Legacy Services LLC](https://www.signalandlegacy.com/) helps amateur-radio operators document their stations while they are active and enjoying the hobby. The work includes identifying equipment, organizing a technical inventory, recording connections and operating context, and keeping that information useful as a station changes.

When an operator becomes a Silent Key, that documentation can help family members and executors understand what is present and make informed decisions about the next steps. It also provides a starting point for conversations with their own advisers.

The practical message for operators is simple: start with an equipment list, label important connections, photograph the station, and tell a trusted family member where the records are kept. Review those records when equipment is added, replaced, or removed.
