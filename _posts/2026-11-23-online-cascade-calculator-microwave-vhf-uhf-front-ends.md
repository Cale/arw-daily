*By Tim Daniels (G7KTP)*

[RF Shack](https://rfshack.com/cascade-calculator/) ([rfshack.com](https://rfshack.com/cascade-calculator/)), the site of UK microwave operator Tim Daniels [G7KTP](https://rfshack.com/author/tim-g7ktp/), has published a free online Cascade Calculator for working out the performance of a complete receive chain.

Users enter each stage in signal order (feed cable, changeover relay, LNA, filter, transverter and so on) with its gain and noise figure, and optionally P1dB and IP3. The calculator then works out the overall gain, noise figure, OIP3 and OP1dB of the whole chain using the Friis noise formula. If the antenna or sky temperature is known, it also gives the full system noise temperature, which is useful for comparing masthead and shack-mounted preamp options or judging how much an LNA really helps your setup.

Lossy passive stages such as cables and relays can be entered as a simple loss, and two worked examples are included to get started. The tool runs in the browser with no sign-up.

The site also offers several other free calculators: an Offset Dish f/D calculator for designing feeds from simple tape-measure readings, a Maidenhead Range &amp; Bearing calculator supporting 10-character locators for microwave portable operation, and a dBm/Watts converter.
