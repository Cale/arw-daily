*The following is a message from the European PSK Club:*

After a long break, the [European PSK Club](https://epcqso.com/) (EPC) QSO Party is returning on 21–22 November 2026 with updated rules and a dedicated [website](https://epcqso.com/).

Radio amateurs worldwide are invited to take part in this 24-hour digital-mode contest.

Contest details:

- **Date:** 21–22 November 2026
- **Time:** 12:00 UTC Saturday – 12:00 UTC Sunday
- **Mode:** BPSK63
- **Bands:** 160, 80, 40, 20, 15 and 10 metres
- **Participation:** Open to EPC members worldwide

The contest features updated scoring, EPC membership number multipliers, multiple entry categories and downloadable certificates.

All participants must have a valid EPC membership number. Membership in the European PSK Club is completely free, and new members can [register](https://www.eupsk.club/home/registration) online before the contest.

**EPC membership registration:**   
[https://www.eupsk.club/home/registration](https://www.eupsk.club/home/registration)

Amateur radio operators of all experience levels are welcome to participate. We also encourage amateur radio clubs and digital-mode enthusiasts to help spread the word.
