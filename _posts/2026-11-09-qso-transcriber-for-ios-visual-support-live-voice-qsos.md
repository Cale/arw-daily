*The following is a message from the creator of [QSO Transcriber](https://qsotranscriber.com/):*

My name is Poul Laustsen, callsign OV9PL, and I’m a radio amateur from Denmark.

I’ve just released an iPhone app called [QSO Transcriber](https://qsotranscriber.com/), which I originally started building because of a problem I experienced myself as a relatively new radio amateur.

During voice QSOs, I found it surprisingly difficult to listen carefully, remember a callsign and signal report, think about what to say next, and operate the radio at the same time. I also experienced some of the microphone anxiety that many newer operators will probably recognize.

QSO Transcriber is intended as a visual support and confidence tool during real radio QSOs.

It can:

- Provide live transcription of voice QSOs
- Detect and highlight possible callsigns and signal reports
- Record QSOs for later review
- Let the operator listen back and correct information before saving it
- Export QSO information in ADIF format
- Process transcription locally on the iPhone rather than sending QSO audio to a cloud transcription service

And a lot more, but it would be quite extensive to have it all here:-).

The goal is not to replace listening, learning or normal operating skills. It is meant to reduce some of the information pressure while operators gain experience and confidence on the air.

I have deliberately tested the app using real radio audio rather than clean studio speech, including weak signals, noise, accents and normal conversational QSOs.

QSO Transcriber is now available in the Apple App Store.
