The [East Coast Reflector](https://www.eastcoastreflector.com/) will provide a series of presentations over Zoom that highlight former NASA mission controllers sharing their experiences. The first presentation will take place Monday November 2nd at 8:00 PM Eastern Time. [The Zoom link is available here](https://ecr.radio/zoom).

Additional presentations will take place over the next several weeks:

**November 2nd**   
Tom Hanchett   
Apollo Lunar Mission — Radio Links

**November 9th**   
Bill Weppner   
Apollo Memories: A Flight Controller's Story

**December 7**   
Bill Weppner   
Mercury, Gemini and Apollo

**Coming Soon**   
KJ5KJ James Kelly

> These aren't simply presentations about history. They're personal stories from those who experienced the challenges and triumphs of America's space program firsthand.
