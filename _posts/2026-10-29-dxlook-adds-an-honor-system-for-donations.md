*The following is a message from [DXLook](https://dxlook.com/):*

About 4,000 hams use [DXLook](https://dxlook.com/) every month. Around 100 of them — roughly 2.5% — help support it through donations. Their generosity, along with what I put into it myself, keeps the site free and available to everyone.

Keeping a project like this going means figuring out how to ask for help without getting in the way. So I’ve added a simple honor system: click “I already donated this month,” and the donation reminder goes away until next month. There are no accounts and nobody checks. If you say you donated, that’s good enough for me.

The reminders are also less frequent now: no more than once a day, and never on your first visit.

To those 100 people helping keep DXLook running: thank you. You’re making it possible for a lot of other hams to use it too.

Rodrigo, AK6FP
