*The following is a message from the creator of Costas:*

[Costas](https://costasdigi.com), a new free and open-source FT8 and FT4 program for Windows, is now available for download. It uses the same decoder as WSJT-X, so it decodes as well and sounds identical on the air. What changes is the screen: instead of a scrolling list of decoded lines, Costas shows one row per station, ranked by what you need for your log, whether the station is free to call, and whether it can hear you.

### Built Around the Question Every FT8 Operator Asks

On a busy band, an FT8 operator spends each 15-second slot scanning dozens of decoded lines to find a station worth calling. Costas does that work for you. Its *Next in line* list puts the stations you need first: new countries, then new bands, grids and calls, worked out from your QRZ logbook. Stations you’ve already worked on the band move to a separate Repeats list, with when and where you last worked them.

Each station also gets a Hears you and Chance rating from live PSK Reporter data. It shows whether that station, or someone near it, is hearing your signal, rated Good, Fair or Long shot.

### One Click, Then It Runs the Contact

Click ""Call"" or press Enter, and Costas runs the standard exchange, showing each step on a progress strip and logging the contact when it completes. Anyone calling you is pinned above the list with an Answer button. Clicking a recognized DXpedition Fox works it the Hound way automatically, SuperFox included.

Costas is built to keep the operator in charge. It never starts transmitting without you, never changes a message silently, and has a transmit watchdog, a band-edge check, a clock check and PTT release on any fault.

### More Features

- Hunt list: the countries, calls, US states or grids you’re after, with an alert when one is heard on your band, or on other bands through PSK Reporter. Alerts never transmit.
- Contests: ARRL RTTY Roundup, Field Day, WW Digi and ARRL Digi exchanges in FT8 and FT4.
- Logging and confirmations: uploads to your QRZ logbook, LoTW (through TQSL) and eQSL as you go, with confirmations synced back. Wavelog, Cloudlog and Club Log are supported too.
- Awards: DXCC, WAS, WAZ and VUCC progress from your own log.
- Maps of who you hear and who hears you, POTA spots, and upcoming DXpeditions flagged in the station list.
- Transmit level that sets itself per band from your radio’s ALC, with live power, ALC and SWR meters.
- Mini mode and a phone remote for operating from a phone or tablet on your home network.
- Works with your other tools: WSJT-X UDP for GridTracker and JTAlert, plus Hamlib, FLRig and network rigctld for radio control.
- PSK31, PSK63 and RTTY in preview, on the same screen.

### Availability

Costas is free under the GPLv3 license and runs on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, with a Linux build in preview. The decoder and radio control come bundled, so WSJT-X doesn’t need to be installed. Setup walks through six steps: station, radio, audio, a transmit test, a clock check and QRZ.
