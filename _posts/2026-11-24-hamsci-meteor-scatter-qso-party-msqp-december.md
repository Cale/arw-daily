HamSCI will host a [Meteor Scatter QSO Party](https://www.hamsci.org/msqp) December 13-15 during the Geminids meteor shower. Hams are invited to participate on 6 and 10 meters using the MSK144 digital mode. HamSCI has a [how to guide](https://www.hamsci.org/node/984) and [operating guidelines](https://www.hamsci.org/msqp-rules) for hams new to working meteor scatter.

> Listen, make QSOs, collect and then upload WAV files of your decodes, contribute to meteor science as we strive to uncover the secrets of meteor scatter (MS) propagation.

A [list of contributors](https://www.hamsci.org/msqp#Chart) is now available for other hams to see who is QSOs and uploading data.
