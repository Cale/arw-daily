*The following is a message from [Association International Radio Amateur](https://www.aira.pt/en/) (AIRA):*

[AIRA](https://www.aira.pt/en/) - [Special Activation, São Jacinto Military Area, Infantry Regiment No. 10](https://www.aira.pt/en/events/ativacao-base-militar-2026). On September 12, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, AIRA will promote the activation of Infantry Regiment No. 10, located in Aveiro. We invite amateur radio operators and enthusiasts to participate in this special activity. The call sign will be [CS5AIRA/M](https://www.qrz.com/db/CS5AIRA).

The activity will take place on the 20 and 40-meter bands, VHF, and UHF, including connections through the LART Lusophone Network, as well as on the DMR Digital Network via Talkgroup 268 and Talkgroup 91 on the BrandMeister platform. More information is available on the [AIRA website](https://www.aira.pt/en/events/ativacao-base-militar-2026).

[AIRA](https://www.aira.pt/) - [Activacao Especial, Area Militar de Sao Jacinto, Regimento de Infantaria Nº10](https://www.aira.pt/pt/events/ativacao-base-militar-2026).  No dia 12 de Setembro de 2026, das 10h00 às 17h00, a AIRA promoverá a ativação do Regimento de Infantaria Nº10, localizado em Aveiro. Convidamos radioamadores e entusiastas a participarem nesta atividade especial. O indicativo será [CS5AIRA/M](https://www.qrz.com/db/CS5AIRA).

A atividade ocorrerá nas bandas de 20 e 40 metros, VHF e UHF, incluindo ligações pela Rede Lusófona LART, bem como na Rede Digital DMR pelo Talkgroup 268 e Talkgroup 91 na plataforma BrandMeister. Mais informações no [site da AIRA](https://www.aira.pt/pt/events/ativacao-base-militar-2026).
