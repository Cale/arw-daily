Users of the [Veritium HFClock](https://veritiumresearch.com/hf-clock/) now have a fix available to keep their HamClock based device functional. N2TBN and AI4Y have teamed up to provide detailed, step-by-step directions aimed at getting Veritium HFClocks working again. The procedure requires removing the rear panel and flashing the device's firmware.

Once the new firmware is applied, users will run through a few brief setup steps and HFClock will continue to operate.

HFClock owners can [download PDF instructions](https://nas.rcreasey.com/AI4Y/HFClock/Flash_Veritium_ESP8266_HFClock.pdf) that describe how to fix their device.
