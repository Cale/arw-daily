*The following is a message from QRZBook:*

[QRZBook](https://qrzbook.net) is a free social network for radio amateurs, now with its own [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.qrzbook.app)!

QRZBook works the way people already use social media, but everything in it is about the hobby. Members post to a feed, comment, message each other, join groups, use the forum and chat in live rooms... and it's free!

The new Android app puts all of that on your phone, laid out like the social apps you already use. Members can post their on air status so others can see who is on, and check the DX cluster in the app.

There are MANY more features, why not come and take a look for yourself?
