Another [SSTV event](https://mastodon.hams.social/@ARISS_Intl/117552931489901017) transmitting from the International Space Station will take place November 9th through the 16th. The theme of Series 35 will be "Space Memories" celebrating the history of the [Space Shuttle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle). 

**Frequency**   
437.550 MHz

**SSTV Mode**   
Robot 36

Viewers can submit recevied images to ARISS through the [ARISS SSTV Gallery](https://ariss-usa.org/ARISS_SSTV/).
