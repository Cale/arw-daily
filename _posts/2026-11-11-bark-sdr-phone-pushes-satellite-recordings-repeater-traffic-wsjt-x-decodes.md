*The following is a message from BG7BAG Radio:*

[BG7BAG Radio](https://bg7bag.com/) has published four short, free how-tos that use an iPhone push app called Bark to tell an SDR operator when something worth hearing has happened, without sitting at the computer.

Each how-to includes a small Python script, on the operator's own recording computer, that pushes through a relay the reader deploys to their own Cloudflare account. The scripts are published on GitHub, and the articles are in English with a Chinese version below.

- Never miss a satellite recording again:   
  [https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-satellite-recording/](https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-satellite-recording/)
- Get pushed only when the repeater talks:   
  [https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-repeater-watch/](https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-repeater-watch/)
- A push when the band opens to you (WSJT-X decode log):   
  [https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-band-opening/](https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-band-opening/)
- Know when a long recording finishes or fails:   
  [https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-recording-status/](https://bg7bag.com/bark-sdr-recording-status/)

They build on an earlier Bark relay setup:   
[https://bg7bag.com/one-app-two-websites-instant-ham-radio-reminders/](https://bg7bag.com/one-app-two-websites-instant-ham-radio-reminders/)
