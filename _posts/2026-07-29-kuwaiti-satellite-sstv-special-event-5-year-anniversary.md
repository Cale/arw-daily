The Kuwaiti satellite [QMR-KWT-2](https://www.orbital-space.com/qmrkwt2) will transmit special SSTV images celebrating the 5 year launch anniversary of the QMR-KWT-1 satellite.

> QMR-KWT 2 builds on the achievements of QMR-KWT 1, which was launched in 2021 as Kuwait’s first education-oriented CubeSat mission. That earlier project provided students with hands-on exposure to satellite development and operations, laying the groundwork for the expanded capabilities of the second mission. ⁠—[SatellitePro Me](https://satelliteprome.com/news/orbital-space-launches-qmr-kwt-2-student-satellite-mission/)

Look for images to be transmitted between 6:30 UTC August 1st and 19:30 UTC August 2nd. The download frequency will be 436.950 MHz.
