*The following is a message from Antennaut:*

[Antennaut](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/eduardomoraton/antennaut) turns a browser tab into a radio shack.

Plug in an RTL-SDR or HackRF, open the page in Chrome or Edge, and you get a receiver, a scanner that tells voice from carrier from DMR, and decoders for ADS-B, AIS, APRS, FT8, SSTV, RS41 radiosondes, 433/868 MHz sensors and LoRa (Meshtastic and MeshCore), all running on your own computer. There is also an offline map, propagation, satellite passes and a logbook with ADIF, in 20 languages. 

Antennaut is a beta by a solo developer in Spain, closed source, written with the help of an AI coding assistant, and tested with an RTL-SDR V4 and a HackRF One. It will be free. A Kickstarter running until January 22, 2027 pays for accounts and sync: [https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/eduardomoraton/antennaut](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/eduardomoraton/antennaut)
