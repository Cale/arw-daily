The 29th annual [Zombie Shuffle](https://www.zianet.com/qrp/ZOMBIE/pg.html) takes place today 1500 to midnight local time. 

> THE ZOMBIE SHUFFLE is an annual afternoon/evening event for QRPers to get on the air, have some fun, and rack up a really big score in spite of what your CW or operating skills may be.

Those expecting to participate should request a zombie number. Rebirth rules apply.
