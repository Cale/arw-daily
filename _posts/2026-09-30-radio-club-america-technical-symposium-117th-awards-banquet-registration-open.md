*The following is a message from the Radio Club of America (RCA):*

Join the [Radio Club of America](https://www.radioclubofamerica.org/) at the [2026 Banquet & Technical Symposium](https://www.radioclubofamerica.org/awards-banquet)—where the brightest minds in wireless communications come together to share ideas, celebrate achievement, and build lasting connections.

Experience thought-provoking technical presentations, engage with industry leaders, explore emerging technologies, and conclude with RCA’s signature awards banquet honoring distinguished innovators. 

> The Radio Club of America Awards Program is an industry premier event. Every year, it is preceded by the day-long Technical Symposium. Both events showcase expertise and achievement in every segment of wireless. 

The 2026 Technical Symposium & 117th Awards Banquet will take place Saturday, November 21st in New York City.

[Registration is now open](https://www.radioclubofamerica.org/content.aspx?page_id=4091&club_id=500767&item_id=3073466).
