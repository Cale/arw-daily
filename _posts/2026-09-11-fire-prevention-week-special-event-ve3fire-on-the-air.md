This year, Fire Prevention Week will be from Sunday October 4th through Saturday October 10th. In Ontario we will be using special event call sign [VE3FIRE](https://www.qrz.com/db/VE3FIRE), operating as a Wild Card station, and in the US there will be stations using the call signs N0F through N9F along with [KF2IRE](https://www.qrz.com/db/KF2IRE), another Wild Card station. All twelve  stations are working to highlight fire safety regarding the charging of lithium-ion batteries during Fire Prevention Week.

This event is not a contest. As we make contact with hams worldwide, this is more of a public service event where we will be giving tips to our contacts about the possible hazards inherent in overcharging lithium-ion batteries and tips on how to use and charge these batteries safely.

While this event is not a contest, a downloadable printed certificate will be available for those who work ten or more of the twelve stations. More about getting the certificate is available on the [hamfire.com](https://hamfire.com/) information site and QSL information is available on the [VE3FIRE](https://www.qrz.com/db/VE3FIRE) QRZ page.
