HamSCI will host a [Meteor Scatter QSO Party](https://www.hamsci.org/msqp) August 12-14. Hams are invited to participate on 6 and 10 meters using the MSK144 digital mode. HamSCI has a [how to guide](https://www.hamsci.org/node/984) for hams new to working meteor scatter.

> Listen, make QSOs, collect and then upload WAV files of your decodes, contribute to meteor science as we strive to uncover the secrets of meteor scatter (MS) propagation.

[Rules and operating guides](https://www.hamsci.org/msqp-rules) are also available.
